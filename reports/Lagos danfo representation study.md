# Keep the yellow, invent the rest

Danfo can represent Lagos convincingly without licensing anything. The strongest danfo cues are either free to use or easy to make original. Real geography and place names, the state-mandated yellow-and-black livery, the street's languages and its sound are free. Vehicle makes, badges, number plates, agency and union marks, brands, songs and real people are not, and must be invented. The recommended build has four parts. First, three original bus bodies proportioned on the VW T3 and on Japanese cab-over vans: a 14-passenger rear-engined **Classic** as the starting bus, a 14-passenger **Cab-over** and an 18-passenger **Long Roof**, all with a left-hand-drive cab and a right-side sliding door. Second, a yellow livery with black bands, composited in a shader. Third, cut-vinyl slogans set partly in the OFL-licensed Danfo typeface, three wear tiers, and invented plates and stickers. Fourth, a Yoruba-led, code-switching conductor who carries most of the culture through clipped stop calls and change-handling. Set the game in an unnamed present that matches October 2026. Some **55,000–75,000 yellow danfos still carry about half of motorised journeys** and outnumber regulated blue-and-white buses by dozens to one, so the August 2026 franchise agreement belongs in the background, not the plot. Almost all of this fits the approved spec as bus selection, paint and horn choices, earned cosmetics, rivals, regular passengers and conductor commentary. Final economy values, a licences/credits row, any spending, editors and selectors need the user's approval. The evidence is thinner than it looks: primary sites were blocked, and most findings came from search summaries. Many beloved specifics have no source at all, including hand signs, horn patterns, dashboard décor, the exact door side, the seat maps and most phrases. A Lagos native reviewer must confirm each of them before it ships. This report is general information, not legal advice, and seven questions need a lawyer.

**How to read the tags.** The research environment blocked nearly every news, government, encyclopedia and legal host. Only GitHub, Google Fonts' repository files, Apple's developer site and a few licence-text mirrors were reachable. A **[read]** tag means the primary text itself was read. Any other claim was seen only in a search-engine summary of the linked page, and must be re-checked on the live page before it gates production. Within those, **[weak]** marks an unclear attribution or a low-quality source. **[inference]** marks reasoning by the researchers or by this report, not evidence. **[LR]** marks an item with no source at all, such as background knowledge, a design hypothesis or a candidate phrase. A Lagos native reviewer must confirm every [LR] item before it ships, and none of them should be described as documented practice. **[verify]** marks a decision-relevant figure to re-check first. Several Nigerian sites print the current date in their page headers, so "undated" means the publication date is unknown. The evidence comes from five research notes, referred to below as the vehicle, livery, operations, IP and precedents notes; where they disagree, the reconciliation table near the end says which source wins. Earlier repository research is cited as the design dossier ([research-and-design.md](../docs/research-and-design.md)) and the build report ([Danfo mobile build and release strategy](Danfo%20mobile%20build%20and%20release%20strategy.md)). They are engineering targets to measure in Phase 0, not results that have been achieved.

## Three original bodies with 14 to 18 seats cover the working fleet

Danfos began in the 1970s as converted Volkswagen Type 2 Kombis carrying about 12 passengers. The name settled on the flat-fronted, rear-engined VW T3 when it arrived in the 1980s ([The Autopian](https://theautopian.com/why-nigerias-danfo-bus-drivers-might-be-the-craziest-and-most-skilled-drivers-on-the-planet)). Sources still treat the T3 as the archetype. An academic article calls the danfo a "Volkswagen Type 2 (T3) minibus with 14 passengers seating capacity" ([UJ journal](https://journals.uj.ac.za/index.php/JCPMI/article/download/554/351)). Red Bull describes "a VW transporter bus, painted in cadmium yellow with two black stripes" ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/ng-en/events/red-bull-danfo-rally/red-bull-danfo-rally-what-is-a-danfo)).

Today's fleet is mixed. A January 2022 feature describes "refurbished, second-hand buses (usually a Volkswagen Vanagon or Nissan Urvan)" ([BellaNaija/Bird](https://www.bellanaija.com/2022/01/bird-lagos-danfo-driver/)). Live 2026 listings in Oshodi and Mushin sell 2009–2010 Toyota HiAce "Hummer" buses as an "18-seater for commercial use" ([Jiji](https://jiji.ng/oshodi/buses/toyota-hiace-2010-white-ujJo3basPsFBNQ1BfFK5BQwd.html)). The HiAce has led Nigeria's minibus sales with over 35% ([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/?p=45857)). **No survey of model shares exists for any year**, so the 2026 split between VWs and Japanese vans cannot be stated. Because the youngest T3s are now decades old, the Japanese share has probably grown **[inference]**. Mazda, Mitsubishi L300, Ford Transit, Peugeot J5 and Mercedes Sprinter bases are unverified. Mazda buses appear only in crash reports, and the L300 appears in one encyclopedia summary.

That evidence supports three original bodies, rather than one hero bus or a dozen. The **Classic** carries the boxy flat-front silhouette that both Lagosians and outsiders read as "danfo". The **Cab-over** stands for the Japanese vans that make the fleet look current. The **Long Roof** gives Work a real trade-off between capacity and handling. Each body serves the player, the rival cast and Work traffic under different liveries, so three meshes do the work of many. Kenya's *Ma3Racer* is the warning: it offered a single generic 14-seater and collected money bags instead of passengers ([Kahawatungu](https://kahawatungu.com/ma3racer-a-new-game-for-the-serious-mathree-lovers-out-there/)).

### Seat maps follow "four on a row, two in front"

The capacity evidence points to a different answer for each body. In a COVID-19 order of March 2020, Lagos cut danfo loading to 8 passengers from the usual 14 ([BusinessDay via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/nigeria/business-day-nigeria/20200326/281633897329458)). Coverage at the time said 14-seaters had "used to carry four passengers on a row" before the cut to three ([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/transport/article/transporters-yet-to-obey-lagos-guidelines-on-covid-19/)). Drivers "rearranged and added extra seats", fitting four to a bench where the factory fitted three, and squeezed two passengers into the front passenger seat ([The Autopian](https://theautopian.com/why-nigerias-danfo-bus-drivers-might-be-the-craziest-and-most-skilled-drivers-on-the-planet)). An older usage citation records "driver and 2 passengers" painted beside the driver's door ([Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.com/wiki/danfo)). An official's reference to "the conventional 18-sitters most Danfos have" has an unclear attribution **[weak]** ([Guardian](https://guardian.ng/news/fear-grips-danfo-drivers-ahead-of-proposed-ban/)), but the 18-seat "Hummer" listings support the larger class.

The proposed maps are therefore **driver plus two on the front bench, then three rear benches of four (14 passengers) for the Classic and Cab-over, and four rear benches of four (18) for the Long Roof**. These are designs derived from the figures above, not documented floor plans **[inference, LR]**. The conductor gets a door-side position that never takes a fare seat, as the dossier already requires. Do not add "overload" events. The spec says occupancy cannot exceed capacity, and four to a bench already shows the squeeze that real danfos add above factory seating.

### The door is on the right because Lagos drives on the right

Nigeria's Highway Code sets right-hand traffic (dossier source S23). That implies left-hand-drive vans, with the driver at front-left and **the sliding passenger door on the bus's right, kerb side** **[inference]**. The left-hand-drive fleet and Nigeria's 1972 switch to right-hand traffic are background knowledge that was not retrieved **[LR]**. This answers the spec's door-side check: every waiting patch, park bay and drop-off segment sits to the bus's right, and boarders walk toward the conductor's door-side zone. Confirm it against reference photos before modelling, and ask reviewers whether any right-hand-drive imports work as danfos.

### Mass should change with every passenger

The sourced performance figures describe slow, heavy, top-heavy vans. A **VW T3 1.6 TD** reaches 100 km/h in 21.5 s and tops out at 127 km/h ([auto motor und sport](https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/oldtimer/alltagsklassiker-vw-t3-platz-fuer-alle-im-kasten-bulli/technische-daten/)), and the **1.7 D** takes 20.0 s with a 130 km/h top speed ([AutoPlenum](https://www.autoplenum.de/auto/vw/t3/bus-1979/1-7-d-57-ps)). The **Nissan Urvan E24**'s TD27 diesel makes 62 kW and 176 Nm, against a 2,750 kg gross vehicle mass and a 1,250 kg payload ([carsales](https://www.carsales.com.au/research/nissan/urvan/1992/window/169be5af-ac43-4ac6-aa9e-84c3c54d4c0e/)). **Toyota HiAce H100** diesels make 77–94 hp ([fastcarcheck](https://fastcarcheck.uk/specs/make/toyota/hiace/292438)). Assuming 65–70 kg per adult and no luggage **[inference]**, a full Cab-over reaches the Urvan's rated gross mass:

| Body | Race: driver and conductor only | Work: full load |
|---|---|---|
| Classic | 1.52–1.57 t | 2.43–2.55 t |
| Cab-over | 1.65–1.73 t | 2.56–2.71 t, against the Urvan's 2.75 t gross |
| Long Roof | 1.71–2.15 t | 2.88–3.41 t |

The build report proposes a RigidBody3D bus of 2,500–3,500 kg ([build report](Danfo%20mobile%20build%20and%20release%20strategy.md)). That matches a loaded Work bus almost exactly, but it is too heavy for a race bus carrying only its crew.

Add each passenger's mass at boarding and remove it at alighting. This is safe because the spec allows transfers only while the bus is stationary. A full bus then accelerates more slowly, brakes longer and rolls more, for documented reasons. Tune Race at crew-only mass, and convey weight through suspension travel and yaw inertia rather than inflated mass. No source gives body roll, braking distance, brake fade or turning circle for any of these vans, so those remain tuning values.

### Budgets: draw calls and download size, not texture memory, constrain the buses

On the floor tier, a Mali-G52 phone with 3 GB of RAM, the build report allows **at most 150 draw calls, 100–200k on-screen triangles, 200 MB of GPU texture memory and a first download of 150 MB or less**. It reserves ASTC compression for the danfo livery, alpha signage and UI, and uses ETC2 for opaque city textures ([build report](Danfo%20mobile%20build%20and%20release%20strategy.md)). A 1024² ASTC 4×4 livery mask costs about 1.3 MB with mipmaps, so texture memory is not the limit; draw calls and download size are. The proposed per-bus targets, to be measured in Phase 0, are about four draw calls at full detail (body, glass, interior and wheels) and one at distance, about 15k triangles for the player bus including its interior and 8–10k for rivals, and one shared livery shader and interior atlas per body **[inference]**. Korope, molue and regulated buses need only low-detail traffic models.

## Dozens of yellow danfos still run for every regulated bus

Estimates of the danfo fleet run from "over 55,000" in a LAMATA project report ([LAMATA LBR report](https://www.lamata-ng.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Final-LBR-Project-Report.pdf)) to 75,000. The 75,000 figure is a LAMATA estimate that ICIR reported ([ICIR](https://www.icirnigeria.org/money-for-the-boys-how-agberos-pocket-billions-of-lagos-transport-revenue/)), repeated by LAMATA's managing director ([Trapeze](https://trapezegroup.africa/resources/transforming-lagos-lessons-in-urban-mobility-from-lamatas/)) and by Nairametrics in August 2026 ([Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/21/lagos-danfo-reform-faces-a-major-test-experts-spot-the-cracks/)). A 2025 World Bank brief says the old danfo fleet still accounts for **52% of motorised journeys** ([World Bank](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/9807eea1f8c4b3fd9199798ca33144f8-0090012025/original/Lagos-2pgs.pdf)).

On the regulated side, LAMATA puts its fleet at "a little over 1,000" against a need of about 15,000 ([Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/?p=510395)) **[verify date]**. The additions so far are 820 Bus Reform Initiative buses between 2019 and 2022 ([World Bank](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/9807eea1f8c4b3fd9199798ca33144f8-0090012025/original/Lagos-2pgs.pdf)), 229 buses on the Lekki–Epe axis in December 2025, about 150 CNG buses plus 20 more delivered in September 2026 ([Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/30/lagos-receives-20-cng-buses-as-fg-targets-lower-transport-fares/)), and a small Yutong electric pilot ([Charged EVs](https://chargedevs.com/newswire/yutong-to-deliver-12000-electric-buses-to-lagos-nigeria)). Even at the low fleet estimate, **dozens of danfos run for every regulated bus [inference]**. A game set "now" should treat yellow danfos as default traffic everywhere except where a specific corridor rule removes them.

Race is unchanged. Its active course holds only competing danfos.

The Lekki–Epe corridor needs a decision. The 2024 plan let danfos meeting Vehicle Inspection Service standards run there as mid-capacity buses ([Gazette NGR](https://gazettengr.com/sanwo-olus-government-clarifies-danfo-ban-in-lagos/)). In February 2026 the state redesignated the axis a "Mass Transit and Regulated Transport Corridor", barred koropes, and headlined the carriageway as "out of bounds for informal transport operators". Its replacement buses "bear the standard Lagos State blue-and-white regulated bus colours, featuring active QR codes within PTCS stickers" ([Nairametrics, 18 Feb 2026](https://nairametrics.com/2026/02/18/lagos-says-lekki-epe-carriageway-out-of-bounds-for-informal-transport-operators/)). Whether yellow danfos may still use the main carriageway in late 2026 is unclear **[verify]**. If Lekki–Epe is built, put blue regulated buses on the main carriageway and let the player's danfo serve the feeder roads. The simpler choice is to leave Lekki–Epe out of the launch district. The 2020 okada and keke ban names 6 LGAs and 9 LCDAs, but the notes do not list which ones, and no 2025–26 enforcement status was found **[LR]**.

### The 2026 franchise transition belongs in the background

In early August 2026, Lagos State, LAMATA and the two drivers' unions, NURTW and RTEAN, signed an MoU after more than 30 months of talks. It creates a framework for moving danfo operators into union cooperatives that hold LAMATA bus franchises across the state's **eight Quality Bus Corridors**, with digital fare collection and a gradual rollout ([Nairametrics, 6 Aug 2026](https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/06/lagos-moves-to-phase-danfo-into-franchise-bus-system/); [Arise TV](https://www.arise.tv/lagos-signs-mou-with-nurtw-rtean-to-transition-danfo-operators-into-regulated-bus-franchise-system/); [MSME Africa](https://msmeafricaonline.com/lagos-begins-bus-industry-transition-programme-to-modernize-public-transport/)). An aggregator says the first phase covers four corridors **[weak]**. No list of corridors, no franchise vehicle specification and **no statewide removal date** was found.

Other 2026 measures are narrower than they sound. LAMATA's "no new diesel" rule took effect at the start of 2026 and was publicised in October, but it covers the regulated fleet only and is "not an immediate ban on every diesel-powered bus operating on Lagos roads" ([Nairametrics, 2 Oct 2026](https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/02/lagos-moves-to-phase-out-diesel-buses-mandates-cng-evs-for-regulated-operators/); [ThisDay](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/10/04/lagos-bars-new-diesel-buses-from-regulated-fleet-mandates-cng-electric-vehicles/)). Regulated buses also stopped carrying goods from 1 June 2026 ([Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/05/19/lamata-suspends-movement-of-goods-on-regulated-buses-across-lagos-from-june-1/)). Lagos has announced the end of yellow buses before: in 2017, when Governor Ambode promised 5,000 new buses ([TheCable](https://thecable.ng/lagos-to-replace-danfo-with-5000-new-buses)), and again in a plan to replace "yellow buses with blue ones" ([Ripples Nigeria](https://www.ripplesnigeria.com/lagos-set-to-replace-yellow-buses-with-blue-ones/)), before Governor Sanwo-Olu said he would "remodel" danfos rather than scrap them ([TheCable](https://www.thecable.ng/?p=549791)). An independent researcher warned in August 2026 that franchising "does not guarantee better service" ([Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/21/lagos-danfo-reform-faces-a-major-test-experts-spot-the-cracks/)). A Kenyan site's claim that the deal will "eradicate" danfos and bar them from highways from late 2026 is not corroborated by any Nigerian report ([Streamline Feed](https://streamlinefeed.co.ke/news/lagos-signs-historic-mou-eradicate-danfo-buses-under-franchise-transit-system)).

The game should therefore show a transition that is underway, never one that is finished. Set it in an unnamed present. Yellow danfos dominate, and blue-and-white buses run on busways and regulated corridors. The transition surfaces only in small details: QR-style stickers on regulated buses, fictionalised and without working codes; a different engine note for CNG buses; a fictional "corridor franchise" poster at a park; and a conductor's or passenger's remark about cards versus cash, or about joining a cooperative. One regular-passenger story can follow a trader who chooses the danfo because regulated buses no longer carry goods. That is a current, sourced reason, used as an authored scene **[inference]**.

Do not use the real programme's name or agencies' logos. Do not depict an outcome, such as a final day or danfos being banned. Do not take a side in the "remodel versus replace" argument. Keeping the setting undated also protects the game: if the real transition moves on during the game's life, a content update can add blue buses to corridors without rewriting the story. The Philippine jeepney games show that a phase-out can be the subject of a whole game, as in *Para Po!* ([Inquirer](https://plus.inquirer.net/?p=249171)). The spec rules that out for Danfo, and a light nod is enough.

## Mandated yellow is free to use; every badge, plate and sticker must be invented

### The livery is a colour rule, so build it as a shader

In 2014 Lagos's transport commissioner warned that commercial passenger vehicles "not painted in the state approved yellow colour with black stripes" could not operate ([Vanguard](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2014/05/lagos-starts-enforcement-commercial-vehicles-re-certification/)). In November 2022, citing the Transport Sector Reform Law 2018, LASTMA was told to impound commercial buses not painted "red or yellow with black stripes". Minibuses, including korope, are yellow; two-door mass-transit buses are red ([Gazette NGR](https://gazettengr.com/lagos-begins-impoundment-of-unpainted-commercial-vehicles/); [Gazette NGR](https://gazettengr.com/?p=139102)). No source says who first mandated yellow. The often-repeated 1990s military-administration origin is unverified. No source gives stripe count, width, position or a paint code. "Cadmium yellow with two black stripes" is a description, not a specification.

A colour every licensed operator must use cannot identify one trader's goods. London's black-cab shape failed as a trade mark ([RPC](https://rpc.co.uk/thinking/ip/court-of-appeal-drives-through-london-taxis-hopes-of-enforcing-protection-for-its-shape-marks)). New York regulates "taxi yellow", but its ownable assets are the city-designed logos and markings ([NYC TLC MOU](https://www.nyc.gov:443/assets/tlc/downloads/pdf/mou_printer.pdf)). **Use yellow with black bands freely. Never copy a seal, sticker or logo [inference].**

Build the livery as one shader per body. Its parameters are a yellow shade on a palette running from deep egg-yolk to sun-faded lemon **[inference]**; a band layout (two waist bands of different widths, one thick band, or bands broken across repaired panels); accent colours for bumpers, wheels and roof; a wear tier; and slogan slots. Store the masks as ASTC, following the build report. Godot's Compatibility renderer has **no decals until 4.8** ([build report](Danfo%20mobile%20build%20and%20release%20strategy.md)), so slogans and wear must be UV-mapped into the livery, not projected. Race scrapes stay as separate authored damage states that clear after the event.

Every player paint choice keeps yellow with black bands, because that is the identity. Players vary shade, band layout, accents, lettering colour and wear. Each rival keeps one fixed scheme. This matches the Kenyan nganya precedent, where individual buses such as "Mood" become recognisable celebrities ([People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/insights/matatu-culture-loud-proud-and-unapologetically-kenyan/amp)), and Bus Simulator Indonesia, where livery is the main way players express themselves (dossier source S36).

### Write new slogans in the documented registers

Slogans are the strongest vernacular cue an original game can generate freely. Inscriptions are "carefully chosen; they tell the character and persona of the owner" ([BellaNaija/Bird](https://www.bellanaija.com/2022/01/bird-lagos-danfo-driver/)). Agbiboa's scholarship describes them as ways danfo workers "get by, define their identity and expand their horizons of possibility" ([Cambridge Core](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-modern-african-studies/article/mobile-bodies-of-meaning-city-life-and-the-horizons-of-possibility/EE4EE15CBAD39D1249A74C80FBAE7C12)).

The documented lines are few. The 2022 BellaNaija feature gives "No King as God" and "No condition is permanent"; "Ola enu kan, omo Iya", which it glosses as "One love, my kin"; "Suru lere", glossed as "patience is a virtue" (closer to "patience is profit", so review it); and, from a Mushin driver-owner, "In Jesus Christ, fire!" and "Blood of Jesus" ([BellaNaija/Bird](https://www.bellanaija.com/2022/01/bird-lagos-danfo-driver/)). "Time Na Money" appears on a danfo in a painting, not a photographed bus ([Art635](https://art635.gallery/?p=1470)). "Jesus is Lord" signals a devout driver nationally ([Rilwan](https://rilwan.substack.com/p/counsellors-men-of-god-and-other)).

**"Mummy's Blessing", "God Dey" and "Lagos no dey carry last" were not found in any source.** The IP note's examples ("God's Time Is The Best", "Monkey Dey Work Baboon Dey Chop", "Alhamdulillah", "Masha Allah") are also undocumented **[LR]**. No Islamic inscription was documented in any reachable source, even though Lagos is religiously mixed **[LR]**. Where slogans are placed on the bus is also undocumented **[LR]**. One essay claims the 2021 Road Traffic Law bans slogans on commercial vehicles, but no statutory text corroborates it ([RPUBLC](https://old.rpublc.com/?p=17169)). The game can present slogans as vernacular without implying anything about their legality.

Short phrases and proverbs are not protected by copyright, so documented lines and new lines in the same registers are both usable **[inference]**. Write new lines in six registers: Christian invocations, Muslim invocations **[LR]**, Yoruba proverbs about patience, destiny and family, Pidgin wit about money, time and hustle, English motivational sayings, and gratitude to a parent or sponsor, which Agbiboa's work mentions ([Makeshift Mobility](https://makeshiftmobility.substack.com/p/40-as-i-was-saying)). A launch set of 30–40 lines, balanced across these registers and reviewed by native speakers, is enough. Each rival gets one fixed slogan that expresses its personality. The player earns slogan sets through play. Add a capacity marking in plain stencil, in original wording.

Two rules protect the sensitive end of this content. First, **religious lines are earned through play only, never offered through the garage_sample or cosmetic_trial rewarded placements**, so faith is never traded for ad views. Second, Quranic Arabic and Bible verses stay off surfaces that get crashed or soiled unless a Muslim or Christian Lagos reviewer approves. Use only short King James Version lines or the team's own paraphrase. Invent every church, mosque, business name and phone number, and make the phone numbers impossible formats.

### The Danfo typeface is OFL-licensed, but one font is not enough

Google Fonts ships **Danfo** under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, with no Reserved Font Name. Its designers are credited as Afrotype, Seyi Olusanya, Eyiyemi Adegbite, David Udoh and Mirko Velimirović, and it was added on **15 March 2024**. It is a single variable file with a custom **ELSH axis running from 0 to 100** ([METADATA.pb](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/danfo/METADATA.pb) **[read]**). Its three main styles, at 0, 50 and 100, "reflect different cut-out shapes inspired by the vinyl cut-out lettering of the public transportation buses in Lagos" ([Afrotype/Danfo](https://github.com/Afrotype/Danfo) **[read]**). Afrotype made it with "Abu, a lettering artist from Ojota Bus Stop" ([Google Fonts article](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/danfo/article/ARTICLE.en_us.html) **[read]**).

The licence obligations are simple: ship the copyright notice and licence text with the app, which a file inside the package satisfies; never sell the font on its own; and never use the authors' names to promote a modified version ([OFL.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/danfo/OFL.txt) **[read]**). Textures and UI text rendered with the font are "documents created using the font" and are not bound by the OFL. METADATA.pb carries a copy-paste slip ("The Tac Project Authors"), so ship OFL.txt verbatim. Avoid **Danfo Std**, the 2018 release from Dá Design Studio: it is a different release with unknown terms.

Two checks remain open. First, the README says the font covers Google's sub-Saharan African character set, but nobody has inspected it for Yoruba diacritics and lowercase letters. The team can check this for free by opening the TTF. Second, real lettering is hand-cut vinyl. One tidy digital font across every slogan would read as fake, so add per-letter jitter, uneven baselines and mixed sizes. Use the three ELSH masters for variety, and mix in original hand-lettering drawn in Krita.

### Three wear tiers express a bus's history, not poverty

Officials and the press call danfos "rickety" and "dilapidated", with "squealing brakes, bald tyres and smoky exhausts" ([Guardian](https://guardian.ng/life/danfo-the-naked-realities-of-nigerian-automobiles/)). The head of the Vehicle Inspection Service called dilapidated vehicles "ticking time bombs" with "corroded body structures" ([Ripples Nigeria](https://ripplesnigeria.com/lagos-targets-ticking-time-bombs-as-it-intensifies-crackdown-on-rickety-vehicles)). In March 2024 VIS inspectors checked brakes, wipers, lights, shocks and tyres, and impounded 50 buses ([PM News](https://pmnewsnigeria.com/2024/03/27/lagos-vis-impounds-50-rickety-commercial-vehicles/)). Generic T3 buyer guidance names wheel arches and the windscreen surround as rust points ([Classics World](https://classicsworld.co.uk/classic-car-price-guide/affordable-vw-campers-buyers-guide/)).

The dossier described "maintained, patched and decorated" looks as creative variations, not verified fleet classes. The new notes ground that idea in three tiers **[inference]**:

| Tier | Body | Lettering and trim | Mechanical read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh | Even deep yellow, crisp bands, clean wheels | New vinyl slogans, intact lights | Clean door slide |
| Working | Sun fade on roof and bonnet, a few dents, one mismatched panel, bands that misalign across repairs | Peeling letters with ghost outlines | Worn, rattling door |
| Battered | Rust at arches, sills and door bottoms; several yellow shades over filler; soot at the exhaust | Missing trim, cracked lenses | Misaligned or rope-tied door, visible smoke |

Wear is an appearance choice in My Bus. It never forces a repair, which the spec forbids. Transient race damage clears after each event, and the player's own history shows through mementos and plates rather than imposed dirt. Saturday PUNCH reported real safety complaints about bus condition in October 2026 (dossier source S32). Wear therefore shows character and craft, never squalor, and the battered tier carries no joke about unsafe conditions.

### Interiors use improvised benches, and the door is unreliable

Many T3-based danfos have had their factory seats replaced by wood or metal benches ([The Autopian](https://theautopian.com/why-nigerias-danfo-bus-drivers-might-be-the-craziest-and-most-skilled-drivers-on-the-planet)). A Lagos writer presents a tidy danfo with "no exposed foam" as the exception ([Lagos Convo](https://lagosconvo.substack.com/p/the-day-a-danfo-driver-surprised)). One passenger recounts a sliding door falling off mid-journey ([WATHI](https://wathi.org/initiative-lagos/contributions-initiatives-lagos/danfo-tales-in-the-big-city)). Build an interior kit of worn vinyl benches with split seams and exposed foam, a bare-plank variant for the battered tier and padded seats for the fresh tier, all on one shared atlas. Players see it through windows and the open door; the spec needs no interior camera.

Sources are silent on dashboard décor, curtains, ceilings, flooring, handholds and sound systems. Prayer items, stickers and toys are plausible earned mementos for the cabin, but none can be called typical until reviewers confirm them **[LR]**. If any are used, include both Christian and Muslim items.

## The conductor, not the bus, carries most of the culture

### The conductor's documented craft

The academic base is solid, but the researchers could reach only abstracts. A study of conductors on Lagos Island and the Mainland finds speech built from "Yorùbá language, Pidgin English, English language, tag-switching and colloquialism", with Yoruba dominant, plus personification and metonymy ([UNILAG, Òjó & Ntekim-Rex 2024](https://ir.unilag.edu.ng/items/61c7d3ce-f8d2-4763-876e-7dbdf1a57f68)). Obiorah (2021) finds that conductors clip stop names through "vowel deletion, nasal deletion, vowel lengthening, consonant deletion, and word deletion", so "new travelers often time miss their bus stops" ([Jurnal Arbitrer](https://arbitrer.fib.unand.ac.id/index.php/arbitrer/article/view/272)). Adedun's 2005 study of 221 recorded interactions calls the language "creative, attitudinal, emotional, group-directed" ([UNILAG](https://ir.unilag.edu.ng/handle/123456789/3065)). Neither the per-stop table nor the recordings were readable.

Accounts of the job describe a conductor chanting stops while running after a moving bus, his arms "a blur of motion, effortlessly directing the passengers into the bus", and walking up to waiting people to ask where they are headed ([Kolawole](https://michaelkolawole.substack.com/p/the-prehensile-and-mind-reading-skills)). At parks, drivers hold passengers while small notes are found to make change ([Ikeja Record](https://ikejarecord.com/?p=3102)). Conductor pay is informal and daily: one was handed ₦3,000 at the end of a 2022 workday, and owners hire and fire conductors weekly ([Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2022/06/25/a-day-in-the-life-of-a-lagos-bus-conductor/)). Door-riding is described in search snippets whose sources could not be told apart **[weak]**.

Several familiar behaviours have **no source at all [LR]**: a destination hand-sign system (and South Africa's minibus-taxi system must not be transplanted to Lagos), banging on the bus body as a stop or go signal, notes folded between the fingers, go or stop words to the driver, and the change-pairing practice one summary calls "junction-marriage". Animate what is sourced: the run-up beside a slowing bus, arm sweeps that funnel boarders in, the turn to ask a waiting person's destination, and change-juggling. Keep the spec's rule that the door closes before departure. Show the conductor at the open door only during the slow approach to a stop and the pull-away from it. That rule is approved, it keeps play readable, and it avoids glamorising a dangerous practice.

### Passengers: "owa", exact change and the middle bench

Passengers are told to "learn to say 'owa' when you arrive at your stop so the driver doesn't miss it". Conductors "are not good listeners", so first-timers should repeat their destination ([Zikoko](https://www.zikoko.com/life/these-7-tips-will-help-you-survive-lagos-danfo-buses/)). That gives the spec's advance drop-off cue a native trigger: the passenger calls "owa", the conductor relays it, and the player stops. The same guide says to "avoid the front row… The middle bench is the sweet spot". It is unclear whether "front row" means the seat beside the driver, so a regular passenger's preference for the middle bench is a flavour choice **[LR]**.

Exact change matters, and big notes cause arguments ([Erico Trips](https://ericotrips.wordpress.com/2018/09/27/5-things-you-should-know-about-danfo-buses/)). "Balance" means change ([Sassy Funke](https://sassyfunke.com/the-travails-of-the-danfo-bus-passenger/)). Fares "often rise without prior notice… mostly in the evening" ([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/?p=1091857)).

Philippine students' *Jeepney Simulator* went viral by modelling change-making, fuel and family costs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeepney_Simulator); [Inquirer Esports](https://esports.inquirer.net/34576/student-game-developers-go-viral-for-their-jeepney-simulator)). Danfo's conductor handles change automatically, but the moment can still be visible and audible: a short change exchange, a grumble about a ₦1,000 note on a short hop, a surge explained in rain. None of it needs a cashier minigame. Regular passengers can draw on the dossier's sourced situations, such as a returned lost item, and on the goods-carrying trader above.

### Parks, agberos and enforcement are fictional, neutral systems

The dossier already separates the conductor from the agbero (park collector or tout). The new notes add the "loading fare", "an euphemism for a park tax" paid at every park ([Ikeja Record](https://ikejarecord.com/?p=3102)), and layered 2022 dues ([ICIR](https://www.icirnigeria.org/money-for-the-boys-how-agberos-pocket-billions-of-lagos-transport-revenue/)). These belong in the existing park-cost line of the shift result. Never show every fee as legitimate tax or every collector as a criminal, and never name real union leaders, whom the reporting involves in allegations. The Lagos Run critique sets the bar: agberos and traffic officers feel authentic only when they change the money and the trip, not when they are reskinned obstacles ([EdgeX FR](https://pro.edgex.exchange/fr-FR/news/article/lagos-run-danfo-browser-game)).

Enforcement is real and increasingly camera-based. LASTMA logged about **38,000 violations from April to June 2026**, with illegal BRT-lane use a focus ([Sahara Reporters](https://saharareporters.com/2026/07/13/lastma-says-agency-recorded-38000-traffic-offences-lagos-three-months)). Driving against traffic carries vehicle forfeiture with "no fine" ([Lagos Ministry of Justice](https://lagosstatemoj.org/?p=6284)). Extortion allegations are contested: LASTMA opened a probe into one in April 2026 and said "the burden of proof rests" on the accuser ([The Mirror](https://themirroronline.com.ng/alleged-%e2%82%a6100000-extortion-lastma-begins-probe-seeks-evidence/)). Violence runs both ways ([Vanguard](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2024/09/traffic-infraction-danfo-drivers-attack-lagos-taskforce-during-enforcement/)).

In Work, show a fictional traffic authority with an invented uniform, camera gantries and a junction officer. Its consequences are time and route penalties, such as being waved off a busway. Contraflow appears only as an explicit, opt-in race hazard, as the dossier proposes. There is no bribery mechanic.

### Characters are full rigs only at the door

The build report renders waiting crowds as vertex-animated MultiMesh instances. Only the two to six passengers actually boarding become skinned characters ([build report](Danfo%20mobile%20build%20and%20release%20strategy.md)). The spec requires each passenger to keep the same identity and appearance through boarding, travel and departure. Both requirements hold if each appearance is an ID, made of a mesh variant plus clothing-atlas indices **[inference]**. A waiting figure starts as a crowd instance, becomes a skinned character for the walk to the door and the step inside, then becomes a seated instance with the same appearance ID, drawn in one MultiMesh per bus, and turns skinned again to alight. The driver and conductor stay skinned. Distant buses show seated silhouettes only. The authoritative seat count never depends on what is drawn.

## No licensable Lagos recording was found, so Danfo must record its own

The documented reference is Emeka Ogboh. His 2022 album *6°30'33.372"N 3°22'0.66"E*, on his Danfotronics label, is built from field recordings at Ojuelegba, including "overheard conversations of conductors and drivers" ([Bandcamp Daily](https://daily.bandcamp.com/features/emeka-ogboh-interview)). It is processed music rather than raw ambience ([Resident Advisor](https://ra.co/reviews/35016)). He has also turned a danfo into a sound sculpture. His recordings are commercial artworks with no open licence. They are listening references only; the IP note adds that they must never be sampled.

"Sound City" lists the go-slow as "street music, revving engines, hawking, shouting directions and taking phone calls" ([The Common](https://thecommononline.org/?p=4912)). **No CC0 or CC-BY Lagos field recording could be verified**. Freesound, Wikimedia Commons and Radio Aporee were unreachable, and searches surfaced nothing. The nearest commercial analogue is a Ghanaian trotro depot pack, which is the wrong city and needs a paid licence ([Artlist](https://artlist.io/sfx/track/soundscapes-of-ghana---trotro-bus-depot-condutor-shouting-stops-street-ambiance/125042)). No source documents which genres or radio stations danfos play **[LR]**. This corrects the dossier, which allowed "voluntary contributions with clear permission" without saying what permission means. The notes make it concrete: written releases.

| Layer | What to capture | Evidence | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic engine | Rear boxer-type note, idle, pull-away, gear changes | Rear engine sourced (dossier S4); the sound itself unsourced | Record a cooperating owner's T3-type bus in ordinary driving |
| Cab-over engine | Diesel under the cab | **[LR]** | Record a HiAce- or Urvan-type bus |
| CNG variant | Different note for bi-fuel buses | CNG conversion sourced ([BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/?p=1063813)); sound unsourced | Optional, later |
| Door and body | Sliding-door rattle and slam, panel buzz, suspension creak | Generic, unsourced | Record; covers the door's wear states |
| Horns | Original player tunes and a signature horn per rival | Patterns and meanings unsourced **[LR]** | Compose and record originals |
| Conductor and passengers | Calls, "owa", change, phone calls, grumbles | Patterns sourced | Volunteer Lagos voice actors under written release |
| Street | Hawking, other conductors, horns, go-slow | Sourced generically | Field ambience at Ojuelegba, Obalende and Oshodi, cleaned |
| Music | Diegetic in-bus radio | Genres unsourced **[LR]** | Original compositions |

The recording plan works within the zero-new-spend rule. **Ambience** comes from the bus parks sound artists already favour, captured with the team's existing phones or recorders. Remove any radio or shop speaker playing a real song, and make every identifiable voice unidentifiable by layering or pitch-shifting unless its speaker signs a written release. Identifiable voices are personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, and a conductor's stylised chant can arguably be a "performance" under the 2022 Copyright Act's performers' rights ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/copyright/1332056/an-overview-of-the-copyright-act-2022); [Templars](https://www.templars-law.com/app/uploads/2026/08/Commercial-Use-of-Individuals-Images.pdf)). **Vehicle sounds** come from a cooperating owner-driver's bus in ordinary conditions, never from staged reckless driving.

**Voices** come from volunteer Lagos voice actors under a written release. It should grant worldwide, perpetual, all-media use in an ad-supported commercial game with editing rights, and it needs an explicit clause on AI voice cloning, a credit preference and NDPA-compliant handling of the recordings. Avoid minors. **Music** is original, in fuji-, juju-, highlife-, gospel- and Afrobeats-inspired styles, with no melody, hook, lyric, sample or soundalike of any real song. Keep out traditional songs, chants and oriki (praise poetry), because the 2022 Act's folklore section carries criminal liability. Keep out CC BY-SA music too, because that licence makes any music synced to moving images an adaptation ([CC BY-SA 4.0](https://github.com/spdx/license-list-data/blob/main/text/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) **[read]**). **Radio stations** are invented. Nigeria's music licensing bodies are in turmoil, with COSON no longer an approved collecting society ([Interspace Music](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/coson-court-of-appeal-mcsn-royalty-collection-nigeria/)), and *Crazy Taxi* shows licensed brands and songs being stripped from later releases ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2010/11/29/review-crazy-taxi/)).

Horns deserve more than a settings slot. Indonesia's "om telolet om" craze made a bus horn a global meme ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2016/12/23/506713677/honk-if-you-love-memes-the-om-telolet-om-phenomenon-explained)), and Bus Simulator Indonesia's players still install new telolet horn packs through its garage sound menu ([LapakGaming](https://www.lapakgaming.com/blog/id-id/others-telolet-basuri-bussid/)). The same craze drew a minister's safety request because children waited at roadsides for honks ([RRI](https://rri.co.id/en/trending/1089964/telolet-rhythmic-bus-horns-when-entertainment-and-danger-collide)). Give each rival a signature horn so players recognise it by ear, and offer earned original horn tunes in My Bus. All horn choices keep the same gameplay effect, as the dossier requires, and nothing rewards honking at pedestrians or children.

Audio is the likeliest budget overrun. The engine library alone takes about 24 MB of the 150 MB first download ([build report](Danfo%20mobile%20build%20and%20release%20strategy.md)). At Ogg Vorbis rates, an hour of mono speech at 32–48 kbps costs about 14–22 MB, 20 minutes of stereo music at about 96 kbps costs about 14 MB, and ambience beds and effects add about 10 MB more **[inference: arithmetic, not measured]**. Cap launch audio at about **35 MB** and test speech bitrates on a floor phone's speaker. Keep music short and diegetic, playing under the existing Sound setting. Record each conductor's clipped stop names once and assemble calls from a stop-name clip plus a call frame, rather than recording a whole call for every stop pair. Reviewers must check that the assembled calls sound natural **[LR]**.

Three conclusions follow.

First, **the undated ₦3,000–₦5,000 owner-delivery figures are stale**. One full 14-seat leg at ₦1,000–₦1,500 grosses ₦14,000–₦21,000, so a ₦5,000 daily remittance would be a trivial share of a 2026 day. That contradicts the sourced accounts of heavy deductions. The notes' own scaled guess of ₦5,000–₦10,000 is still low. Treat delivery as a share-based tuning value instead **[inference]**.

Second, **fuel makes the load-and-go decision real**. An illustrative 12 km medium leg costs about ₦2,100 in petrol-class fuel. That is 12.5% of a full Classic's ₦16,800 gross at ₦1,200 a seat, but 25% of a half-full bus's ₦8,400. This is the approved informed-work-decision feature in action. Diesel's 2026 doubling makes the cost bite harder for diesel-class buses.

Third, tune the shift result so take-home pay is visibly a minority of gross fares during hire purchase and rises noticeably when instalments end. That fits the 2015 report that about half of takings went on dues and the spec's promise that ownership improves take-home pay.

Show surge conditions on the Work job card as disclosed "conditions", never as punishment. Keep fuel, conductor pay, park dues and the owner payment as separate lines in the existing result breakdown. Never fold park dues into hire-purchase progress.

## Real places, colours and speech are free; makes, marks, music and people are not

Store policy, not case law, is the binding risk for a zero-budget team. Apple's guideline 5.2.1 bars "protected third-party material such as trademarks, copyrighted works, or patented ideas in your app without permission". Guideline 2.3.7 bars trademarked terms in metadata, and 2.3.9 requires rights to everything in icons and screenshots ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) **[read]**). Google Play runs an impersonation policy alongside its trademark complaints ([GoodBarber](https://www.goodbarber.com/help/google-rejection-r101/google-rejection-intellectual-property-infringement-a223)). A complaint can get the app pulled first and argued later.

US case law protected realistic Humvees in *Call of Duty* ([Finnegan](https://www.finnegan.com/en/insights/blogs/incontestable/in-legal-warfare-over-humvee-trademarks-the-first-amendment-goes-beyond-the-call-of-duty-in-dismissing-am-generals-claims.html)). That defence is US-only, it helps only once a lawsuit has started, and it narrowed after *Jack Daniel's* (2023) ([Proskauer](https://www.proskauer.com/blog/in-jack-daniels-case-supreme-court-rejects-ninth-circuits-expansive-view-of-first-amendment-rogers-test)). *Textron v EA* shows that prominence in marketing raises the risk ([The Licensing Letter](https://www.thelicensingletter.com/?p=796)).

Licensing is a poor fit regardless of budget. Carmakers vet damage models ([Jalopnik](https://jalopnik.com/why-car-damage-sucks-in-racing-games-developer-explains-1850060288)), and Toyota once said it did not want its cars in games that promote illegal street racing ([The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/29510/toyota-backtracks-on-why-it-wont-license-cars-to-video-games-like-need-for-speed)). The sharpest vehicle risk is Volkswagen's: the EUIPO accepted the T1 "Bulli" shape as an inherently distinctive 3D trade mark in 2021, citing its **V-shaped front bead** ([MLL News](https://www.mll-news.com/the-shape-of-the-vw-bulli-a-trademark-a-trademark/?lang=en)). VW has used its German T1 mark against a model-car maker, and the final outcome of that case is unconfirmed ([BGH via rewis](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/zq5-02-05-2024-i-zr-2323/)).

Two licence duties need no new menu. The OSM notice should read **"Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) — openstreetmap.org/copyright"**. Show it on the navigation map and the loading screen, and add it to the store description. The cleaned road graph the team keeps is a Derivative Database, so shipping the game obliges the team to publish that dataset, or its transformation scripts, free of charge ([ODbL](https://github.com/spdx/license-list-data/blob/main/text/ODbL-1.0.txt) **[read]**; [OSM copyright text](https://github.com/openstreetmap/openstreetmap-website/blob/master/config/locales/en.yml) **[read]**). The game's code and art stay proprietary.

The dossier's reference photos are CC BY-SA 4.0: Wole Hammond's danfo (source S5), the Ojuelegba underbridge (S8) and Lagos Marina (S11). They stay reference only. A texture made from them would be an adaptation bound by ShareAlike, and app-store DRM arguably conflicts with the licence's ban on technological measures **[inference]**.

### Inspired-by vehicle design checklist

There is no legal bright line; this is practitioner-style judgement.

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Name | Fictional marque and model, searched on the free WIPO Global Brand Database and USPTO. No "VW", "Volkswagen", "Kombi", "Bulli", "Transporter", "Toyota", "HiAce", "Nissan" or "Urvan" in game text, store listing, keywords or patch notes. "Danfo", "korope" and "minibus" are fine |
| Badge | Original shape and letterform, unlike the VW roundel and the Toyota and Nissan emblems |
| Front face | Original grille, headlamp shape, indicator placement and bumper. On the Classic: no split or bay windscreen, no V-shaped front bead or panel, no round central badge |
| Proportions | Blend at least two references and invent details; round the dimensions so no single model is replicated |
| Lights | Original tail-lamp and headlamp signatures, since tail-lamp shapes are commonly registered as designs |
| Wheels and cabin | No maker's logos on hubs, steering wheel or dashboard **[inference]** |
| Source hygiene | Modelled from many photos used only as reference; no photogrammetry, photo projection, traced lettering, or purchased or ripped models |
| Recognition test | Lagos reviewers and playtesters call it "a danfo" and cannot name the make and model unprompted; otherwise revise |
| Marketing | Icon, screenshots and trailers show only bodies that pass; no real vehicle serves as the game's identity |
| Record | References and decisions logged in the source/licence register |

## Lagos reviewers must clear 29 groups of unsourced items before they ship

The review process costs nothing. Recruit two to four volunteer Lagos-based reviewers, mixed in gender, including both Christians and Muslims, with at least one Yoruba speaker and at least one regular danfo commuter. Give them the bus models, livery sheets, slogan list, script and barks, signage, audio stems and a capture video of each district. ## Conclusion

For Danfo, authenticity and IP safety point the same way. The details that make a danfo read as Lagosian cannot be licensed from anyone anyway: the wear, the slogans, the conductor's clipped calls, the ratio of yellow to blue on a given road, the moment a passenger says "owa". What is restricted is a carmaker's face, a telecom logo or a hit song, and those are the details outsiders lean on when they lack local knowledge. A team that invests in the free, specific details gets both representation and legal cover. A team that leans on recognisable products gets neither. The binding constraints are therefore not legal. One is epistemic: the most loved details are exactly the unsourced ones, so the volunteer Lagos review loop is a production dependency, as real as the device lab. The other is technical: audio, not art, is the likeliest place to break the 150 MB budget.

The 2026 transition changes what "representative" means over the game's life. Today, a city of yellow danfos with blue buses on its corridors is accurate. In two years it may be a portrait of a moment, and a stable, undated setting that content updates can adjust ages better than one that tries to predict the outcome. A game in which the player works toward owning a danfo, released just as Lagos begins moving danfo operators into franchises, will be read by Lagos players as commentary whether or not it intends to be. Neutral, affectionate specificity, voiced by insiders and kept away from any verdict on the policy, is the safest and most respectful position available.
