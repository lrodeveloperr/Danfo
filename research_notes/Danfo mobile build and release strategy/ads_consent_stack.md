## 1. Mediation platforms in 2026: market position, bidders, 2025–2026 changes and Nigeria/Africa performance

### Takeaway
In 2026 the field is AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay and Google AdMob mediation. MAX dominates top ad-monetised games, but no published data shows any platform winning in Nigeria or Africa. Credible Nigeria eCPM figures by format are not public. Proxies put Nigeria at roughly $0.25–0.80 blended CPM, an order of magnitude below tier-1. For Danfo the deciding factor is the engine. On Godot, AdMob mediation (bidding) through the actively maintained Poing plugin is the low-risk path, with AppLovin, Unity/ironSource, Mintegral, Pangle, Liftoff, InMobi, Moloco and Meta added as bidders inside it. On Unity 6, MAX or LevelPlay become viable, and an A/B test should decide between them.

### Inferences
- No published head-to-head shows MAX, LevelPlay or AdMob earning more in Nigeria. Choosing on "Africa performance" now would rest on vendor claims. The defensible approach is to pick the platform with the best **engine integration risk profile**, then add as many relevant bidders as size and stability allow. Once traffic exists, test bidder mixes with AdMob mediation A/B tests (or MAX/LevelPlay A/B tools on Unity).
- Unified bidding has largely closed the gap between the platforms. With AdMob mediation, AppLovin, Unity/ironSource, Mintegral, Pangle, Liftoff, InMobi, Moloco, Meta and DT Exchange demand can all compete inside AdMob's auction. With MAX or LevelPlay, Google demand competes through partner bidding. What remains is the "home" demand each platform privileges and its UA tie-ins, which matter little to a game that does no paid UA.
- For Nigeria specifically, Google's own demand is likely the deepest local demand: AdMob Network plus Google Ads advertisers targeting Nigeria. Mintegral, Pangle and Bigo are typically strong in emerging markets. This argues for AdMob mediation on Godot. It is an inference, not a measured result.
- Order-of-magnitude revenue expectation (illustrative arithmetic only, not a benchmark): a Nigeria blended eCPM of $0.30–3.00 at 2–4 full-screen or rewarded impressions per DAU gives ARPDAU of about $0.0006–0.012. Global players from tier-1 countries will be worth 10–30× more per impression, so the "global" share of traffic will drive revenue disproportionately.

### Gaps
- No verifiable Nigeria or Africa eCPM by format (rewarded vs interstitial) and OS from 2025–2026 was found. Tenjin's Q2 2026 country table and Appodeal's country tables may contain it but could not be opened (blocked). The only Nigeria numbers available are low-quality blended CPMs.
- Neither AdMob's current "Choose ad sources" page (bidding/waterfall flags per network as of Oct 2026) nor MAX's or LevelPlay's current bidder lists could be fetched. Moloco and LINE bidding on AdMob is inferred from Poing adding those adapters in July 2026 and Google's July 2025 announcement.
- Unverified: whether AppLovin acts as a *bidder* (not only waterfall) inside AdMob mediation in Oct 2026.
- No independent 2026 market-share update (post-March 2025) was found.

## 2. Engine integration: official SDK support for Godot vs Unity, community plugins, and a minimal custom bridge

### Takeaway
Google provides **no** official Godot SDK. The community **Poing Studios godot-admob-plugin** is mature and actively maintained: v5.1.0 on 13 Sep 2026, Godot 4.4/4.5+ tested through 4.7.2, GMA Next-Gen Android SDK 1.4.0 and iOS 13.9.0. It ships adapters for 19 mediation partners, UMP with a privacy-options form, SSV options and impression-level revenue. AppLovin has an **official** MAX Godot plugin, but it is stale: its last release was 1.2.0 on 24 Apr 2025, with open, unresolved Godot 4.4/4.5 iOS build and initialisation bugs. Unity LevelPlay has no Godot SDK. On Unity 6, all three platforms have first-party plugins.

**Building a custom native bridge (Godot)**
- [V] Android plugin **v2 architecture** (Godot 4.2+; v1 is deprecated). A plugin is an **AAR** depending on the Godot Android library, with an init class extending `GodotPlugin` declared in its manifest via `org.godotengine.plugin.v2.[PluginName]` meta-data. Methods are exposed with `@UsedByGodot` and signals via `getPluginSignals()`/`emitSignal()`. Packaging uses an `EditorExportPlugin` with `_get_android_libraries` (AAR paths) and `_get_android_dependencies`/`_get_android_dependencies_maven_repos` (e.g., `com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads` or `com.applovin:applovin-sdk` plus adapters). Manifest changes go through `_get_android_manifest_*` hooks. **Gradle build is required.** — [godot-docs: Android plugins](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/platform/android/android_plugin.rst)
- [V] An iOS plugin is a static `.a` or `.xcframework` built against Godot headers (SCons-generated), with `[PluginName].debug.a`/`.release.a` variants. A `.gdip` config file gives the plugin name, binary and init/deinit functions. Its `[dependencies]` section covers linked/embedded/system frameworks, capabilities, files, linker_flags and **`spm_packages`** (URL, exact version, products). A `[plist]` section injects Info.plist keys. The plugin is exposed via `Engine.get_singleton()`. The page does not mention CocoaPods. — [godot-docs: iOS plugins](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/tutorials/platform/ios/ios_plugin.rst)
- [V] Godot's iOS export preset has privacy-manifest fields: required-reason API reasons (active keyboard, disk space, file timestamp, system boot time, user defaults), `privacy/tracking_enabled`, `privacy/tracking_domains`, and 35 `privacy/collected_data/<category>/{collected, collection_purposes, linked_to_user, used_for_tracking}` entries. — [godot-docs: EditorExportPlatformIOS class reference](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/classes/class_editorexportplatformios.rst)

### Inferences
- **Godot recommendation:** use Poing godot-admob-plugin v5.1.x pinned to a specific Godot 4.5/4.6/4.7 template, with AdMob mediation bidding. It is the only Godot path that is (a) maintained monthly in 2026, (b) on Google's preferred Next-Gen Android SDK, (c) using SPM on iOS, and (d) shipping UMP, the privacy-options form, adapters for the main emerging-market bidders, `on_ad_paid` and SSV hooks. Its main risk is a single maintainer team (Poing/Patreon-funded). Mitigate by pinning versions and testing every Godot upgrade against the plugin's template matrix.
- **The MAX Godot plugin is not production-safe for iOS on Godot 4.5+ today**, given unresolved iOS init and build bugs since Sept 2025 and no release since April 2025. It also needs manual Gradle/AAR and Podfile steps on every export. A team wanting MAX on Godot should budget for forking it or writing its own bridge.
- **What a minimal custom bridge needs (MAX or AdMob):** Android: one Kotlin `GodotPlugin` (about 300–600 LOC) exposing init, consent, load/show for interstitial and rewarded, and the revenue callback, with signals for loaded/failed/shown/dismissed/rewarded/paid. All SDK calls must be marshalled onto the Android UI thread and signals emitted back. An export plugin then declares Maven dependencies (SDK + adapters + UMP) and manifest meta-data (APPLICATION_ID/SDK key). iOS: an Objective-C++/Swift static xcframework with a `.gdip` declaring `spm_packages` (GoogleMobileAds, GoogleUserMessagingPlatform, adapters; MAX distributes via CocoaPods/SPM). Plist entries cover GADApplicationIdentifier or AppLovin key, `SKAdNetworkItems` and `NSUserTrackingUsageDescription`. Plus the privacy-manifest fields in the export preset. The ongoing cost is tracking SDK and adapter versions monthly, which is exactly the work Poing already does for AdMob.
- **Unity 6 recommendation if the engine changes:** all three SDKs are first-party and current. MAX (8.6.x) is the market default with the largest demand graph and built-in consent-flow tooling, LevelPlay is Unity's own path, and the GMA Unity plugin with AdMob mediation remains viable. Unity removes the plugin-maintenance risk, so the engine decision (covered elsewhere) dominates.

## 3. Consent and privacy: UMP/TCF (EEA/UK/CH), US states (GPP), ATT, SKAdNetwork/AdAttributionKit, Nigeria NDPA 2023 + GAID 2025

### Takeaway
To get personalised ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Danfo must show a **Google-certified, IAB-TCF CMP**. Google's free UMP SDK is certified, and TCF v2.3 strings have been mandatory since 28 Feb 2026, which UMP writes automatically. UMP also handles US-state opt-outs (GPP) and an IDFA explainer before ATT. Asking for ATT is cheap and worth it in an ads-only game: about 39% of prompted iOS gamers opted in, per Adjust's Q1 2026 data. It should be shown after a value moment and never incentivised. Nigeria's NDPA 2023 and GAID 2025 apply extraterritorially to anyone targeting Nigerian users. They require a compliant privacy notice, a lawful basis (in practice consent for ad tracking), parental consent for under-18s, and possibly NDPC registration once Nigerian users exceed the "major importance" thresholds. Those thresholds are commonly cited as 200+ data subjects in 6 months, but they conflict between sources and need legal confirmation.

### Cited Findings
**EEA/UK/Switzerland: certified CMP + TCF**
- [V] Under Google's EU User Consent Policy, publishers using AdMob must use a **Google-certified CMP integrated with IAB TCF** to serve personalised ads in the EEA and UK from **16 January 2024**, and in **Switzerland from 31 July 2024**. "When these requirements are not met, you will not be eligible to serve personalized ads." Certification does not equal legal compliance. Google still supports its Additional Consent spec for non-TCF vendors. — [AdMob Help 13554020](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/13554020?hl=en) (content via search results)
- [V] **TCF v2.3**: the IAB mandatory implementation date was **28 February 2026**. The Disclosed Vendors segment became mandatory. TC strings created after that date without it are invalid, while pre-deadline strings remain valid. Google systems accept v2.3 strings (from Oct/Nov 2025, sources differ) and **Google's own CMP writes v2.3 automatically**. Third-party CMP users had to coordinate with their vendor. — [IAB Europe transition page](https://iabeurope.eu/all-you-need-to-know-about-the-transition-to-tcf-v2-3/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-mandates-tcf-v2-3-migration-by-february-2026/). A vendor claims that missing it drops inventory to "Limited Ads" — [Getconzent](https://getconzent.com/blog/iab-tcf-23-certified-cmp-the-2026-guide-for-compliant-publishers/) [Vendor]
- [V] The IAB CMP API v2 spec changelog includes "February 2026 … Updated TCData to use disclosedVendors". In-app CMPs store `IABTCF_CmpSdkID`, `IABTCF_gdprApplies`, `IABTCF_TCString`, `IABTCF_PurposeConsents`, `IABTCF_VendorConsents`, `IABTCF_DisclosedVendors` and others in SharedPreferences/NSUserDefaults. Vendor and mediation SDKs read `IABTCF_gdprApplies` and `IABTCF_TCString`. "When gdprApplies is 1, mediation runs only among GDPR-ready ad network SDKs." If a CMP is removed, the publisher must clear the `IABTCF_*` keys. — [GitHub IAB TCFv2 CMP API v2.md](https://github.com/InteractiveAdvertisingBureau/GDPR-Transparency-and-Consent-Framework/blob/master/TCFv2/IAB%20Tech%20Lab%20-%20CMP%20API%20v2.md)
- [V] Google ad-serving modes under TCF: **personalised ads** need consent for Purposes 1, 3 and 4, plus legitimate interest or consent for P2, P7, P9 and P10. **Non-personalised ads** need consent for P1 plus LI/consent for P2, P7, P9 and P10, and still use identifiers for frequency capping and aggregate reporting. **Limited ads** need only Special Purposes 1–2 (plus optional LI on P7), with no local identifiers. Vendor consent for Google is required too. — [Poing mirror of Google "ad serving modes"](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/blob/master/docs/privacy/ad_serving_modes/ad_serving_modes.md)
- [V] The UMP flow: create the message in AdMob Privacy & messaging, then call `update()` (requestConsentInfoUpdate) **on every launch** without relying on cached strings. Load the form, and show it if status is REQUIRED. Debug geography lets you test EEA/non-EEA, and `reset()` is for testing only. — [Poing mirror of UMP get-started](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/blob/master/docs/privacy/user_messaging_tools/get_started.md)
- [B] UMP requires apps to offer a **privacy options entry point** (re-open consent) when `privacyOptionsRequirementStatus == REQUIRED`. Only call `MobileAds.initialize`/load ads once `canRequestAds()` is true. — [developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy). Poing v5.0.0 implements `present_privacy_options_form` ([V] [release notes](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/releases/tag/v5.0.0)).

**US state privacy laws (GPP)**
- [V] Google supports IAB GPP for applicable US states. The SDK honours GPP signals in local storage, and **UMP writes only `IABGPP_HDR_GppString` and `IABGPP_GppSID`** (e.g., section 7 for US National). Create a "U.S. state regulations" message in AdMob. **RDP** can be set globally with the `gad_rdp` key (Android int 1 in SharedPreferences, iOS bool in UserDefaults) or per request with extras `rdp=1`. With mediation, manage US-state ad partners in Privacy & messaging and check each partner's options. Test with debug geography `REGULATED_US_STATE`. Setting `tag_for_under_age_of_consent` on UMP stops it asking that user, but UMP does **not** forward TFUA to the ads SDK. — [Poing mirror of Google US-states guide](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/blob/master/docs/privacy/regulatory_solutions/us_states_privacy_laws.md); [Google AdMob US states (cn mirror)](https://developers.google.cn/admob/ios/privacy/us-states?hl=en)

**Apple ATT, IDFA explainer, SKAN/AdAttributionKit**
- [V] UMP can show an **IDFA explainer message before the system ATT prompt**. This needs the AppTrackingTransparency framework linked and `NSUserTrackingUsageDescription` in Info.plist. The UMP form loads only when the ATT status is "not determined", and the prompt shows once per install. — [Poing mirror of IDFA guide](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/blob/master/docs/privacy/user_messaging_tools/idfa_support.md)
- [V] **ATT opt-in rates (Adjust):** gaming apps reached **39% in Q1 2026** among users shown the prompt, up from 38% in Q1 2025. Sports (54%) and action (50%) were highest. India rose from 44% to 51% and Indonesia was 60%. In 2025, US gaming opt-in was about 32%. Adjust's Q1 2025 sources disagree (37.9% vs 39%). — [Adjust ATT opt-in rates](https://www.adjust.com/blog/att-opt-in-rates-2025); [Adjust gaming report via GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/adjust-mobile-games-insights-report)
- [B] App Review Guideline 5.1.2(i) says apps must request ATT permission before tracking. They must not gate functionality or offer incentives for granting it, and must not use other identifiers to track users who decline. — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- [B] Publisher apps should list ad networks' `SKAdNetworkItems` identifiers in Info.plist (AdMob, MAX and LevelPlay publish lists) so attributable install campaigns can bid on non-IDFA iOS traffic. **AdAttributionKit** (iOS 17.4+) is Apple's successor framework and is interoperable with SKAdNetwork. Ad networks and advertisers handle the postbacks, and the publisher's role is the Info.plist identifiers and showing ads via the network's SDK. — [Apple AdAttributionKit docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/adattributionkit); [AdMob iOS quick start (SKAdNetwork)](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/quick-start)

**Nigeria: NDPA 2023 + GAID 2025**
- [V] The GAID was issued **20 March 2025** and took **effect 19 September 2025**. It reaches "a data controller or processor who targets a data subject in Nigeria, regardless of whether [it] is domiciled or resident in Nigeria." — [NDPC GAID PDF](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf) (via search summaries); [Banwo & Ighodalo synopsis](https://www.banwo-ighodalo.com/grey-matter/synoptic-analysis-of-the-nigeria-data-protection-act-general-application-and-implementation-directive-gaid-2025/)
- [V] **Registration** applies to data controllers/processors of **major importance**. Secondary sources commonly cite a threshold of **200 data subjects within six months**, with tiers such as Ultra-High Level (>5,000 data subjects) filing annual **Compliance Audit Returns by 31 March**. The six-month period runs from when a threshold is crossed. **Conflict:** the GAID Article 8 text defines major importance by reference to "a number set by the Commission", not a fixed 200, so check the primary text. — [Mondaq "Unlocking GAID 2025"](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1684204/unlocking-gaid-2025-answers-to-all-your-burning-questions); [NDPR Toolkit 2026 guide](https://ndprtoolkit.com.ng/blog/ndpc-registration-guide-2026/); [StackWeaver CAR guide](https://stackweaver.cloud/insights/ndpc-compliance-audit-return/); [O3 Legal briefing on audit returns and enforcement](https://o3legal.com/briefing-data-protection-audit-returns)
- [V] **Children under the NDPA:** a child is defined by reference to the Child's Rights Act, i.e. **under 18**. Processing a child's data requires **parent or guardian consent**, with "appropriate mechanisms to verify age and consent" (s.31; one aggregator cites s.33). Section 31(5) empowers the NDPC to make regulations for **children aged 13+** who directly request online services, but no evidence was found that these have been issued. — [Mondaq NDPA key provisions](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/data-protection/1366934/the-nigerias-data-protection-act-2023-a-look-at-key-provisions); [S.P.A. Ajibade review](https://spaajibade.com/?p=4132); [LinkedIn commentary (Ehi Akpata)](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/ehi-akpata-2a672196)
- [B] **NDPA 2023 core obligations** (Act signed 12 June 2023):
  - lawful bases (s.25), with consent required to be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous and withdrawable (s.26);
  - information to data subjects or privacy notice (s.27);
  - DPIA for high-risk processing (s.28);
  - controllers of major importance must designate a DPO (s.32) and register with the NDPC (s.44);
  - the right to object, including to direct marketing (s.36);
  - cross-border transfer conditions (ss.41–43);
  - fines for major-importance controllers of the higher of ₦10m or 2% of annual gross revenue, and for others the higher of ₦2m or 2% (s.48). — [NDPC: Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023](https://ndpc.gov.ng/resources/) (section numbers from prior knowledge; verify)
- [B] NDPC's February 2024 Guidance Notice on registration set tiers: Ultra-High (>5,000 data subjects in 6 months; ₦250,000), Extra-High (>1,000; ₦100,000) and Ordinary-High (>200; ₦10,000). These combine with sector criteria. — [NDPC](https://ndpc.gov.ng/) (from prior knowledge; the fees and tier mapping conflict across secondary sources)

### Inferences
- **UMP is the right CMP for Danfo on either engine.** It is free and Google-certified. It writes TCF v2.3 and GPP keys that mediated SDKs read, and it handles the IDFA explainer. On MAX, AppLovin's consent flow integrates Google UMP [B, unverified this session]. On LevelPlay a separate TCF CMP is needed (UMP works).
- **Offline-first interaction:** UMP needs connectivity to refresh consent, and ads cannot load offline anyway. Call `update()` at launch when online. When offline, skip ads silently, as the spec already says ("no inventory/offline/failure: continue"). Never block gameplay on the consent form. Put the "Privacy options" entry in **Settings**: the spec allows "Settings: controls, sound, subtitles and camera comfort", so the menu-scope owner must approve one extra row.
- **ATT is worth asking in an ads-only game.** At about 30–40% opt-in among prompted gamers, IDFA-available impressions typically command higher bids, and the cost is one system dialog. Show the UMP IDFA explainer after the first completed event (a value moment), never at launch, with no incentive (5.1.2). iOS will likely be a small share of Nigerian traffic but a larger share of global tier-1 traffic, which is where ATT pays off.
- **Nigeria practicalities for a no-backend game:** embedded ad SDKs collect advertising IDs, IP addresses and interactions on the publisher's behalf. Danfo's publisher is therefore probably a controller of Nigerian users' personal data, even without its own servers. Likely minimum compliance:
  - a public privacy policy covering NDPA s.27 items and naming ad partners;
  - a consent prompt for personalised ads to Nigerian users (UMP can be configured for "other regions" or a custom message; alternatively serve non-personalised ads in Nigeria by default);
  - treat users who declare under 18 as children (non-personalised or limited ads, no profiling);
  - monitor Nigerian user counts against the NDPC registration thresholds (200+/1,000+/5,000+ in 6 months), which an ads-funded game with any traction will quickly pass, so budget for registration and CAR filing.

  This needs a Nigerian data-protection lawyer's confirmation.
- The NDPA's under-18 child definition is stricter than COPPA's under-13 and GDPR's 13–16. This shapes the age-screen design in Q4.

## 4. Child-directed / mixed audience: COPPA, Play Families, neutral age screens, child-directed flags

### Takeaway
The safer choice for Danfo is a **13+ target audience** in Play Console (no under-13 age groups), a matching store listing and content rating, and no child-attractive marketing. Add a **neutral age screen** in-game, used only to route ad treatment. Choosing "Designed for Families" or including under-13 groups would trigger the full Families policy: Families Self-Certified ad SDKs only, no interest-based ads or AAID, and the program is not accepting new SDK applicants. That would severely cut eCPM and narrow the mediation options. Because Nigeria defines children as under 18, under-18 Nigerian users also need non-personalised ads.

### Cited Findings
- [V] Play Families policy: a **mixed-audience app must include a neutral age screen** so that children see only non-personalised ads. Apps for children and older users must not use APIs or SDKs not approved for child-directed services unless they sit behind a neutral age screen or collect no children's data. Apps whose audience is **only children must use Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs**, and ads shown to children must come from those SDKs. Certified ad content must avoid interest-based advertising and remarketing to children. — [Google Play Families Policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335?hl=en); [Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12918983?hl=en); [Manage target audience and app content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159?hl=en)
- [V] The Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program "is currently not accepting new applicants". Existing participants' requirements are unchanged. — [Play Console Help 12955712](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12955712?hl=en)
- [V] **COPPA amendments**: published in the Federal Register on 22 April 2025 and **effective 23 June 2025**. Regulated entities had to comply by **22 April 2026** (except §312.11(d)(1), (d)(4), (g)). On **25 February 2026** the FTC issued a policy statement limiting enforcement when mixed- or general-audience services collect children's data **solely for age verification**, on conditions: use limited to verification, security, deletion afterwards, and notice in the privacy policy. — [Federal Register 2025-05904](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule); [Finnegan/Mondaq "COPPA's amended rule is now in full effect"](https://www.finnegan.com/en/insights/articles/coppas-amended-rule-is-now-in-full-effect-what-operators-need-to-know.html); [Davis Polk](https://www.davispolk.com/index%2Ephp/insights/client-update/ftc-prioritizes-coppa-enforcement-new-compliance-obligations-take-effect)
- [V] AdMob request flags: `tag_for_child_directed_treatment = TRUE` treats the request under COPPA. It disables interest-based ads and remarketing and stops AAID/IDFA transmission, and misuse can lead to account termination. `tag_for_under_age_of_consent = TRUE` (EEA users under the age of consent) disables personalised ads and remarketing, blocks third-party vendor requests and stops AAID/IDFA. **Child-directed takes precedence** when both are set. `max_ad_content_rating` (G/PG/T/MA) caps ad content to match the app's rating, per Play's Inappropriate Ads policy. — [Poing mirror of Google targeting doc](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/blob/master/docs/advanced_topics/targeting.md)
- [V] In the 2026 Google SDKs, `TagForChildDirectedTreatment` and `TagForUnderAgeOfConsent` are **deprecated in favour of a new `AgeRestrictedTreatment` setting** (GMA Unity plugin v11.3.0, 19 Jul 2026). — [GitHub googleads-mobile-unity releases](https://github.com/googleads/googleads-mobile-unity/releases)
- [V] A third-party guide (not Google policy text) cautions that a neutral age screen "is not a decorative date-of-birth prompt": it must not nudge users towards an older age. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/davekurian/google-play-families-policy-build-checks-for-child-targeted-apps-23pa)
- [B] Play Console's target-audience declaration determines whether the Families policy applies. Google may still treat an app as appealing to children based on its store listing and content even if under-13 is not selected. Including under-13 groups makes the app subject to the Families policy, though not necessarily the "Teacher Approved" programme. — [Play Console Help 9867159](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159?hl=en)
- [B] Apple's Kids Category (Guideline 1.3) bans third-party advertising and analytics except in limited, human-reviewed circumstances. Ads must be appropriate for the app's age rating (2.5.18). In 2025 Apple introduced new age-rating tiers (13+, 16+, 18+) with a 31 Jan 2026 deadline to answer the updated questionnaire (needs verification). — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

### Inferences
- **Recommended configuration (safer choice):**
  - Play target audience: 13–15, 16–17 and 18+ (no under-13).
  - Do **not** opt into Families or Kids Category.
  - Content and store creatives aimed at teens and adults.
  - `max_ad_content_rating` set to T on Play, matching the IARC rating; an M/MA ad rating suits only a 17+/18+ rating.
  - A **neutral age screen** (year-of-birth picker with no default and no "you must be 13+" hint) at first launch. It routes ad treatment:
    - **under 13**: set child-directed / `AgeRestrictedTreatment`, skip UMP and ATT, serve only non-personalised/limited ads, ideally Google-only or certified demand (or no ads);
    - **13–17**: TFUA/age-restricted where GDPR age-of-consent applies (13–16 by EEA country), and non-personalised ads in Nigeria (NDPA under-18 rule);
    - **18+**: normal UMP/ATT flow.

  This mixed-audience-capable design is cheap insurance in case Google deems the game child-appealing. Collect age only for this routing, store it locally, and disclose it in the privacy policy. That matches the FTC's Feb 2026 age-verification enforcement statement and is consistent with "no backend".
- A driving game with a Lagos bus-owning hire-purchase story is plausibly teen-appealing but not child-directed. Its Lagos danfo setting, loan and career themes and lack of cartoon branding support a 13+ position. Avoid cartoon-styled icons or mascots in store art.
- "Designed for Families" would remove most mediation partners and personalised demand. In Nigeria's already low-eCPM market it would likely make the game financially unviable.

## 5. Platform ad policies: Play interstitials/Better Ads, AdMob rewarded rules, Apple rules, ads declaration, app-ads.txt, Data safety, nutrition labels, privacy manifests

### Takeaway

- [V] Google Mobile Ads SDK Data safety disclosure: the SDK automatically collects and shares **IP address** (approximate location), **user product interactions** (launches, taps, video views), **diagnostics** (launch time, hang rate, energy) and **device or other IDs** (Android advertising ID, app set ID). The purposes are advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Users can reset the ad ID, and the manifest can block ad-ID collection.   - full-screen video interstitials **before the app's loading screen**;
  - full-screen interstitials that **cannot be closed after 15 seconds**.

- [B] **AdMob rewarded ads policy:**

Data safety and a privacy policy URL are mandatory. — [Play Console Help: Ads declaration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455); [Advertising ID](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6048248)

  - ads must be appropriate for the age rating;
  - users must be able to see the information used to target them;

### Inferences
- **The Danfo spec's pacing already complies.** Ads show at results screens after the outcome is saved: first after 3 events and 180 s, then 2 events, 180 s since the last ad and 120 s active play. Launch, driving, pause, menu, background-return and quit are excluded. This is the "after the score page" pattern Play explicitly allows. Keep the interstitial close button available within 15 s or rely on the natural-break exemption, and never chain two full-screen ads.
- **The naira naming risk:** Danfo's in-game currency is called "naira (₦)", a real currency name. AdMob prohibits real-money rewards. Reward prompts and the privacy/terms text should say it is **fictional in-game naira with no cash value, non-transferable and non-redeemable**, to avoid a reviewer misreading "earn ₦500 for watching" as a cash reward. Cosmetic rewards are lower risk.
- **Free website for app-ads.txt and the privacy policy:** a GitHub Pages user site (`<user>.github.io`, root of its own registrable domain because github.io is on the Public Suffix List) can host both at zero cost. This should be confirmed with the AdMob crawler before launch. A project-page path (`/repo/app-ads.txt`) will **not** work.
- **Data safety for Danfo** (no backend): declare the ad-SDK data (approximate location via IP, app interactions, diagnostics, device IDs; shared with third parties for advertising and analytics; encrypted in transit). Declare the local-only age entry only if it leaves the device. Add each mediated SDK's disclosure: each adapter adds its own data types.
- **iOS export:** fill the Godot privacy-manifest fields for the engine's own required-reason API use (e.g., UserDefaults and file timestamps). Rely on the GMA, UMP and adapter SPM packages' own manifests, and merge everything into the App Store privacy label. Set "Used for tracking" true for Device ID and Advertising Data if ATT-authorised personalised ads are served.

## 6. Ad implementation best practices: preloading, frequency capping, timeouts, offline, SSV vs client rewards, revenue measurement

### Takeaway
For an offline-first game with no backend:
- **preload one interstitial and one rewarded** after consent and initialisation;
- enforce Danfo's own pacing **client-side** on top of AdMob frequency caps;
- treat no-fill, timeouts and offline as a silent skip;
- grant rewards **client-side** on the earned-reward callback, exactly once and persisted. SSV needs a server Danfo doesn't have, and single-player cosmetic rewards don't justify one.

Measure revenue with AdMob/MAX reporting plus the `on_ad_paid`/impression-level callback (ARPDAU = ad revenue ÷ DAU). Firebase/GA4 on Godot needs a native bridge because no maintained Godot Firebase Analytics SDK was found.

### Cited Findings
- [V] **SSV** is optional. Google calls the publisher's server with `ad_network`, `ad_unit`, `custom_data`, `key_id`, `reward_amount`, `reward_item`, `signature`, `timestamp`, `transaction_id` and `user_id`. The server verifies the ECDSA-SHA256 signature using public keys from `gstatic.com/admob/reward/verifier-keys.json`, which must not be cached for more than 24 h because they rotate. Google retries up to 5 times at 1 s intervals if the server doesn't return 200. The client `on_user_earned_reward` callback works without SSV. In the Godot plugin, set `ServerSideVerificationOptions` (user_id/custom_data) on the loaded ad before show. — [Poing mirror of Google SSV doc](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/blob/master/docs/advanced_topics/ssv.md)
- [V] **Impression-level ad revenue**: enable it in the AdMob UI. Then attach `on_ad_paid` as soon as the ad object exists (before show). `AdValue` has `value_micros` (5,000 = $0.005), `currency_code` and `precision` (UNKNOWN / ESTIMATED / PUBLISHER_PROVIDED / PRECISE). With mediation, optimised sources report ESTIMATED and others PUBLISHER_PROVIDED. Bidding sources in test requests report UNKNOWN with value 0. Response info gives the ad source name/ID, adapter class, latency and mediation group/A-B test labels. Forward the data to analytics or MMPs (Adjust, AppsFlyer, Singular, Tenjin) yourself. — [Poing mirror of Google ILRD doc](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/blob/master/docs/advanced_topics/impression_level_ad_revenue.md)
- [V] **Initialization before load**: wait for `MobileAds.initialize` completion (adapter status map) before the first load so all bidders join. — [Poing mediation get-started](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/blob/master/docs/mediate/get_started.md)
- [V] Next-Gen SDK **preloading APIs**: the GMA Unity plugin v11.4.0 added `PeekAdResponseInfo()` "for preloading APIs". — [GitHub googleads-mobile-unity releases](https://github.com/googleads/googleads-mobile-unity/releases)
- [V] Nothing in GodotNuts/GodotFirebase (a GDScript Firebase SDK covering auth, database, Firestore, storage and functions) shows Analytics support. — [GitHub GodotNuts/GodotFirebase](https://github.com/GodotNuts/GodotFirebase)
- [B] AdMob: loaded interstitial and rewarded ads expire after a time (about 1 hour; app-open ads 4 hours), so reload stale ads. Use exponential back-off on load failures, keep only one request in flight per format, and use AdMob ad-unit **frequency capping** as a backstop. When the AdMob app is **linked to Firebase** and the Firebase Analytics SDK is present, `ad_impression` events with value are logged automatically (for MAX you log `ad_impression` manually with `ad_platform = "appLovin"`). — [AdMob Android interstitial guide](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/interstitial); [AdMob–Firebase linking](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9755590); [Firebase: measure ad revenue](https://firebase.google.com/docs/analytics/measure-ad-revenue)

### Inferences
- **Recommended Danfo ad-state machine** (fits the spec's "one ad request in flight; grant exactly once"):
  1. Launch, then UMP `update` and the form if required. If `canRequestAds`, initialise (background-thread optimised), wait for the init callback, then preload one rewarded and one interstitial.
  2. On an eligible results screen, show the interstitial only if it is already loaded *and* Danfo's pacing counters pass. Otherwise skip with no spinner or wait. Reload after dismissal.
  3. For rewarded, show the offer button only when an ad is loaded. Persist a pending-reward record with a transaction ID *before* show. Grant once on the earned-reward callback, and reconcile on the next launch if the app was killed mid-ad. Never grant on dismissal alone.
  4. Offline: skip preloading. Retry on connectivity change or at the next results screen with back-off (e.g., 30 s → 60 s → 120 s → cap 10 min). Use a load timeout of about 8–10 s so a hanging auction is abandoned without blocking the UI.
  5. Apply the AdMob frequency caps as an upper bound, slightly looser than Danfo's own caps so the client counters stay authoritative.
- **SSV vs client-side:** Danfo has no backend, no PvP economy and no cash-value rewards, so fraud only affects the cheater's own save. Client-side grants are proportionate. Adding a free serverless SSV endpoint would contradict "no backend" for little gain.
- **Measurement without a backend:**
  - Minimum: AdMob (or MAX) dashboard reports for revenue by country, format and network, eCPM, fill and show rate, plus Play Console and App Store DAU, to compute ARPDAU and impressions per DAU.
  - Better: forward `on_ad_paid` (value, currency, precision, ad source, format, placement name) to Firebase Analytics as `ad_impression`. GA4 BigQuery export would then give ARPDAU and placement-level ROI. On Godot that needs a small Firebase Analytics native bridge (same pattern as Q2), because AdMob–Firebase auto-logging requires the Firebase SDK to be in the app.
  - Firebase Analytics is free but adds an SDK and another Data safety entry.
- Track per placement: show rate (shown ÷ eligible), rewarded opt-in rate, impressions per DAU, eCPM by country/format, and D1/D7 retention by ad-exposure cohort. These test whether the spec's pacing is too tight or too loose.

- Could not verify AdMob's current ad-expiry durations or Next-Gen preloading semantics (whether Next-Gen auto-reloads preloaded ads) from official docs on 18 Oct 2026.
- No maintained Godot Firebase Analytics plugin was confirmed. A targeted search was not possible after the budget ran out.

## 7. Effects of ad SDKs on app size, startup, ANRs and crash rates (Android vitals), and mitigations

### Takeaway
Ad SDKs, and especially many mediation adapters, are a leading source of ANRs and WebView-related crashes and add several MB each. Mitigations:
- initialise late (after first frame or consent) with the SDK's background-thread optimisation (on by default in Poing's plugin);
- keep the adapter set to the few bidders that win in Danfo's geos;
- enable R8 (now built into Poing v5.1);
- use the ad inspector and Play vitals to prune adapters that crash.

### Cited Findings
- [V] The Poing plugin has "Disable Initialization Optimization" and "Disable Ad Loading Optimization" settings (Project Settings → Admob → General → Android). Optimisation is **on by default** and makes the SDK run initialisation and ad loading **on background threads**. — [Poing optimize_initialization.md](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/blob/master/docs/optimize_initialization.md)
- [V] Poing v5.1.0 added `enable_r8_optimization` and embedded consumer ProGuard rules in its AARs, so code shrinking works without manual rules. It also fixed iOS NSNull crashes in failure callbacks (#501) and pause-on-background focus handling. — [Poing releases](https://github.com/poingstudios/godot-admob-plugin/releases)
- [V] The GMA Unity plugin notes that on the Next-Gen Android SDK `MobileAds.Initialize()` must be called on the main thread (v11.3.0). The SDK then does its work internally. — [GitHub googleads-mobile-unity releases](https://github.com/googleads/googleads-mobile-unity/releases)
- [V] Google claims lower ad-request latency for Next-Gen (banner up to 27%, internal Aug–Sep 2025 data). — [Google Developers (de)](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/migration/benefits?hl=de) [Vendor claim]
- [V] The MAX Godot plugin requires changing Godot's activity `launchMode` from `singleInstance` to `singleTask`, an integration change that can affect activity behaviour and should be tested. — [AppLovin-MAX-Godot README](https://github.com/AppLovin/AppLovin-MAX-Godot/blob/master/README.md)
- [B] Android vitals "bad behaviour" thresholds (Play): user-perceived **crash rate 1.09%** and **ANR rate 0.47%** overall, and **8%** on any single device model. Exceeding them can reduce store visibility and show warnings on listings. — [Android vitals docs](https://developer.android.com/topic/performance/vitals)
- [B] AdMob's manifest flags `com.google.android.gms.ads.flag.OPTIMIZE_INITIALIZATION` and `OPTIMIZE_AD_LOADING` move initialisation and loading work to background threads to cut ANRs (the same mechanism the Poing setting wraps). Ad Inspector shows per-adapter latency and errors. — [AdMob Android: optimize initialization and ad loading](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/optimize-initialization)

### Inferences
- **Godot specifics:** Godot's main loop and GDScript run off the Android UI thread, while the plugin marshals SDK calls. Keep the defaults (optimisation on). Call `initialize` after the first scene renders and after UMP, not in an autoload `_ready()` before the first frame. That avoids launch-time hitches and the "ad at launch" policy risk.
- **Adapter budget:** start with AdMob plus about 4–6 bidders likely to matter in Nigeria and other emerging markets and in global tier-1: AppLovin, Unity/ironSource, Mintegral, Pangle, Liftoff, plus Meta or InMobi or Moloco, then test. Each adapter adds an SDK (several MB of APK/AAB and IPA in many cases), its own WebView or video player, and its own crash surface. Remove any adapter that shows up in vitals crash clusters or wins under about 2–3% of impressions. Use Play AAB delivery and R8, and measure download size per release. Download size matters for Nigerian players on prepaid data (inference; see the device and market research elsewhere).
- **Background-return:** the spec excludes ads on "returning from background". Make sure no adapter auto-shows app-open ads, i.e. no app-open ad units are created. Handle the iOS pause-on-background fix in Poing v5.1.0 so the game doesn't resume under a full-screen ad.
- **Monitoring:** use Play Console Android vitals filtered by SDK/library stack frames and Firebase Crashlytics (free; needs another native bridge on Godot) or Play's built-in crash reports. Use AdMob Ad Inspector in debug builds. Gate each new SDK version behind a staged rollout.

- Not verified: the current Android vitals thresholds (no change since 2023 is assumed) and the exact Next-Gen SDK threading model.
